Indonesia Chicken Vegetable Soup (Sop Ayam)

Description

Sop Ayam is a light and comforting Indonesian chicken soup filled with vegetables such as carrots,, potatoes, and cabbage. It is commonly served as a home-style dish and enjoyed with rice, fried shallots, and lime.

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken pieces
  • 1.5 liters water
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1/2 teaspoon white pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

Optional toppings:

  • Fried shallots
  • Lime wedges
  • Chili sauce

Stepsss

  1. Boil the chicken
    In a pot, boil the chicken with water until the chicken becomes tender.
  2. Prepare the seasoning
    Heat cooking oil in a pan and saute the crushed garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the seasoning to the soup
    Pour the sauteed garlic into the chicken broth.
  4. Add the vegetables
    Add carrots and potatoess first. Cook for about 10 minutes.
  5. Add the remaining vegetables
    Add cabbage, celery, and green onions.
  6. Season the soup
    Add salt and white pepper to taste. Let it simmer for a few minutes.
  7. Serve
    Serve the soup hot and garnish with fried shallots and lime juice if desired.
